# CPG Sales Volume Forecasting

## Task

Estimate the projected annual sales volume of CPG products.

## Variable Description

- **`Customer`** — customer identification number
- **`Volume`** — annual sales volume
- **`Office`** — the customer's geographic affiliation with a given region or location
- **`Trade_channel`** — the type of the customer's retail outlet (café, retail chain, convenience store)
- **`Territory`** — the identification number of the territory to which the customer belongs (e.g., Siberia, the Urals, etc.)
- **`Cust_Hier`** — customer hierarchy, e.g., the customer's affiliation with the "Pyaterochka" chain
- **`Price_Group`** — the customer's price category
- **`Customer_Classific`** — a customer classification attribute
- **`CDE_doors`** — the average number of coolers at the outlet for a given customer
- **`Visit_Frequency`** — the frequency of visits to the customer's outlet by a sales representative (per month)
- **`Potential`** — another customer ranking attribute:
  - `IR` — virtually no potential
  - `BR` — low potential
  - `SI` — medium potential
  - `GO` — high potential
  - `PL` — max potential
  - `New Customer — potential not determined
  - `Other` — potential not determined
  - `NOT SEGMENTED` — potential not determined
